## 5. Tone + structure assessment

### What's working well (preserve)

- **"Mission control for AI agent fleets"** — clear, concrete, technical-exciting; the right positioning for the developer-operator audience
- **"Built for what comes after observability"** — sharp positioning vs LangSmith/Langfuse/Phoenix; explicitly tells visitors which category to put us in
- **"Your agents stop hallucinating; they look it up"** — visceral, memorable, instantly communicates the KG value
- **Specific numbers as proof points** (when fresh) — beats fluffy adjectives
- **Self-host vs Cloud framing** is appropriately bilateral (acknowledges both paths matter)
- **PricingPage FAQ tone** is honest (acknowledges what's not yet shipped, e.g. SOC 2 roadmap)

### What's underutilized

- **The fleet/bare-metal story is buried.** Most AI orchestration competitors (LangChain Smith, Phoenix, etc.) stop at conversation observability. Powernode goes all the way down to running the underlying VMs + nodes. This is the deepest moat and barely shows above the fold.
- **The platform-spawning story isn't told.** Federation P9.x lets a Powernode platform spin up another fully-federated Powernode platform. That's a "wait, what?" moment that no competitor offers.
- **No demo video or operator screenshots.** Visitors can read what we do; they can't see it.
- **Blog has zero posts** ("First post coming soon"). Even one launch post would establish editorial voice + give visitors a reason to subscribe.

### Three structural variants for HomePage (recommendation menu later picks one)

**Variant 1 — Keep current** (no structural change, just numeric + URL fixes)
- Lift: ~30 min
- Pros: zero risk, ships fast
- Cons: leaves excitement gaps on the floor

**Variant 2 — Light reorder + capability excavation** (keep structure; add 2 new value cards above fold, replace 2 weakest)
- Lift: ~3 h
- Pros: highest ROI; preserves what works
- Cons: still doesn't tell the bare-metal / fleet story prominently

**Variant 3 — Full restructure into problem → solution → proof arc**
- Hero (problem) → "Observability shows what your agents did. We decide what they're allowed to do next."
- Section 2 (solution) → 3 pillars: Control plane, Knowledge layer, Fleet substrate
- Section 3 (proof) → Numbers + screenshots + tutorials
- Section 4 (deployment options) → Self-host vs Cloud
- Section 5 (CTA) → Waitlist + GitHub
- Lift: ~8 h
- Pros: tells the full story; positions fleet/bare-metal as a primary pillar
- Cons: most work; needs to be reviewed by you before shipping

   excavation per hour (~2 h).

reality + closes the most-visible gaps.

(~10 h additional) to tell the bare-metal/fleet/spawning story
